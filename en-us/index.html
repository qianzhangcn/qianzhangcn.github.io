👨‍🏫 Biography

Qian Zhang, Ph.D. in Engineering, is an Associate Professor and supervisor of master's students, an IEEE Member, a member of the China Institute of Communications, and a member of the CSIG Technical Committee on Transportation Video. In June 2026, Qian Zhang received the Ph.D. degree in Engineering from Shandong University through a direct-entry doctoral program, under the supervision of Prof. Ju Liu (Grade II Professor), with Prof. Zheng Dong as co-supervisor. In 2024, with support from the China Scholarship Council, Qian Zhang undertook joint doctoral training at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, under the supervision of Prof. Yong Liang Guan (Vice President) and Prof. Chau Yuen (IEEE Fellow).

Current research interests include intelligent metasurfaces, convex optimization theory, and artificial intelligence algorithms for wireless communications and sensing. Qian Zhang has published more than 30 research papers in leading journals, including IEEE TWC and TCOM, and major conferences, including IEEE ICC and ICASSP, with 18 papers as first author, co-first author, or corresponding author. Two first-authored papers have been recognized as 🏆 ESI Highly Cited Papers. One first-authored paper was ranked among the Top 2 Most Popular Papers of the Year in IEEE Communications Letters. Four papers were ranked among the Top 50 Most Popular Papers in the monthly rankings of IEEE TVT, WCL, and CL, comprising one first-authored paper, two co-first-authored papers, and one second-authored paper. Three patents have been granted.

Academic service includes membership on the inaugural Young Editorial Board of China Communications (English Edition) and service as IEEE PIMRC 2026 TPC Chair. Qian Zhang has also served as a Technical Program Committee (TPC) Member for international conferences including IEEE ICC, GLOBECOM, and WCNC, and regularly reviews for more than ten international journals, including IEEE JSAC, TWC, TCOM, WCM, TIFS, TCCN, TVT, TITS, IOTJ, WCL, and CL.

As a core research team member, Qian Zhang has participated in several major national and provincial research projects, including the National Key Research and Development Program of China, the General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and the Key Research and Development Program of Shandong Province (Major Scientific and Technological Demonstration Projects). Honors include Outstanding Doctoral and Bachelor's Thesis Awards, Outstanding Graduate of Shandong Province, Outstanding Graduate of Shandong University, two National Scholarships for Doctoral Students, a National Scholarship for Undergraduate Students, the 2026 Academic Star Award of Shandong University (sole recipient in the school), and the 2026 Outstanding Graduate Research Achievement Award of Shandong University (sole recipient in the school). Other honors include first-class scholarships for all four undergraduate years and more than ten national- and provincial-level awards in innovation, entrepreneurship, and discipline-specific competitions.
