👨‍🏫 Biography

Qian Zhang received the Ph.D. degree in Engineering and is currently an Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor with the School of Computer and Communication Engineering, Northeastern University at Qinhuangdao. He is an IEEE Member, a member of the China Institute of Communications, and a member of the CSIG Technical Committee on Transportation Video.

He received the Ph.D. degree from Shandong University in June 2026 through a direct Ph.D. program, under the supervision of Prof. Ju Liu, with Prof. Zheng Dong serving as the co-supervisor. From November 2024 to November 2025, he was supported by the China Scholarship Council as a visiting Ph.D. student with the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, under the supervision of Prof. Yong Liang Guan, Vice President of NTU, and Prof. Chau Yuen, IEEE Fellow.

His current research interests include intelligent metasurfaces, convex optimization theory, and artificial intelligence algorithms for wireless communications and sensing. He has published more than 30 academic papers in leading journals and conferences in wireless communications, including IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, IEEE Transactions on Communications, IEEE ICC, and ICASSP. Among these publications, 18 papers were authored as first author, co-first author, or corresponding author.

Two of his first-authored papers were selected as 🏆 ESI Highly Cited Papers. One first-authored paper was ranked among the Top 2 Most Popular Papers of the Year in IEEE Communications Letters, while four papers were ranked among the Top 50 Most Popular Papers in IEEE TVT, WCL, and CL. He has also been granted multiple invention patents.

He currently serves as a member of the inaugural Young Editorial Board of China Communications and as TPC Chair of IEEE PIMRC 2026. He has served as a TPC Member for international conferences including IEEE ICC, GLOBECOM, and WCNC. He also regularly serves as a reviewer for more than ten IEEE journals, including IEEE JSAC, TWC, TCOM, WCM, TIFS, TCCN, TVT, TITS, IoTJ, WCL, and CL.

As a core research member, he has participated in several major national and provincial research projects, including the National Key Research and Development Program of China, the General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and the Key Research and Development Program of Shandong Province.

His honors include Outstanding Doctoral/Bachelor's Thesis, Outstanding Graduate of Shandong Province, Outstanding Graduate of Shandong University, National Scholarship for Ph.D. Students twice, National Scholarship for Undergraduate Students, Academic Star of Shandong University 2026 (the only recipient in the school), and the Outstanding Graduate Research Achievement Award of Shandong University 2026 (the only recipient in the school). He has also received more than ten national- and provincial-level awards in innovation, entrepreneurship, and academic competitions.
