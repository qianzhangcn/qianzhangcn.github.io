## Research Group

Welcome to our research group.

Our group focuses on advanced wireless communications, integrated sensing and communication, UAV networks, communication-sensing-control integration, and artificial intelligence for wireless systems.

## Research Interests

- Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)
- UAV Communications and Networking
- Communication-Sensing-Control Integration
- Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)
- Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces (SIM)
- Artificial Intelligence for Wireless Communications
- Multi-UAV Cooperative Systems
